Supply Chain Tool

Inventory System

An advanced inventory tracking and management solution. It enables real-time monitoring of stock levels, sales tracking, and comprehensive reporting to streamline overall efficiency.

Stock Tracking

Real-time updates on available inventory and stock levels.

Supply Chain

Optimize orders, deliveries, and prevent out-of-stock scenarios.

Reporting

Generate detailed insights and predictive analytics.

Project Overview

Technologies Used

  • Python 3: Application Logic
  • Tkinter: Dashboard Interface
  • SQLite3: Inventory Data Storage
  • Data Visualization: Analytical reporting

Problem Solved

Small to medium businesses struggle with manually tracking product stock, leading to unexpected shortages. This system automates the supply chain, alerting managers of low stock and maintaining accurate transaction records.

Key Highlights

  • Real-time tracking of incoming and outgoing products.
  • Vendor and supplier management module.
  • Automated low-stock threshold alerts.
  • Comprehensive historical sales data and reporting.
