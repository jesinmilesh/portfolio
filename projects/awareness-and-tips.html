Project Overview

Technologies Used

  • HTML5 Semantic Elements: Accessible structure for modular training modules.
  • Modern CSS3: Glassmorphism styling, responsive flexbox/grid, and fluid micro-transitions.
  • Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+): Dynamic DOM rendering, state management, and real-time form validation.
  • Client-Side Engine: Zero-latency questionnaire evaluation running entirely in the browser.

Problem Solved

Human error is implicated in over 85% of cybersecurity breaches. Traditional security policies are often ignored due to dense, non-interactive formats. This tool delivers bite-sized, gamified cybersecurity hygiene training with immediate diagnostic self-checks.

Key Highlights

  • Multi-topic selector with instant DOM switching for 6 fundamental security topics.
  • Instant security score algorithm grading answers against recommended cyber best practices.
  • Color-coded risk bands: 🟢 EXCELLENT (100%), 🟡 GOOD (80%), 🟠 NEEDS IMPROVEMENT (60%), and 🔴 HIGH RISK (≤40%).
  • Transparent diagnostic breakdown highlighting both secure habits and vulnerable practices.

Curriculum Vectors

  • Password Security: Entropy, passphrase uniqueness, MFA, and manager adoption.
  • Phishing Protection: Domain verification, pretexting traps, and malicious attachments.
  • Safe Browsing: HTTPS validation, certificate inspections, and suspicious downloads.
  • Mobile & Wi-Fi: Public hotspot encryption, rogue captive portals, and permission sandboxes.
